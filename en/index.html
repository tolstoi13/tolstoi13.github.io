About

Four ways to pour a beer

We pour the Czech way. Tell the bartender how you feel today:

Hladinka

The classic: a dense two-finger head of foam with beer underneath. Our default pour.

Šnyt

Half a portion in a big glass: beer, foam and air. For trying a new tap or “one more and that’s it”.

Mlíko

A glass of soft, sweetish foam. Drink it in one go before it settles.

Čochtan

Almost no foam, beer to the brim. For those who ask “no foam, please”.

History

Tolstoy really
walked these streets

In 1851 young Leo Tolstoy came to the Caucasus and settled in the German colony of Neu-Tiflis — today's Chugureti district. He rented rooms in a house on Mikhailovsky Avenue (now 52 Davit Aghmashenebeli Avenue) and it was there that he worked on his first novel, “Childhood”.

The street that bears his name crosses that avenue just 150 metres from his house. For over two months Tolstoy strolled these Neu-Tiflis blocks daily, walked to the Kura river and crossed the Vorontsov bridge into the city centre. That very Vorontsov Square appears on the hand-painted mural by our taps — a 19th-century view: our street begins right behind the square.

Tolstoi Street itself didn't exist yet — but his boots most certainly trod this ground. Now this is where we pour.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book a table?

On weekdays there's usually room; on Fridays, Saturdays and karaoke Wednesdays better call +995 544 99 79 39 or message us on Instagram @bartolstoi13.

What should I get with my beer?

The classics — smoked wings or fish, from anchovies to squid. With a big group, go for the pork ribs: the portion is about a kilo.

Do you have non-alcoholic drinks?

Yes: non-alcoholic beer (ginger and APA), iced teas — chamomile-mango, peach, mango-pineapple — fruit drinks, tea and Turkish coffee.

Do you take cards?

We take cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Cash lari is welcome too.
